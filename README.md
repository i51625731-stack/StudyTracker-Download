# StudyTracker 🔥

A study tracker that turns learning into a daily habit.

- 🔥 **Daily streaks**: study every day and keep your flame alive
- ❄️ **Streak freezes**: missed a day? A freeze saves your streak
- 🏆 **12 achievements**: from First Steps to Study Legend
- 📊 **Statistics**: see where your study time goes

Free · Works offline · Your data stays on your PC

## Troubleshooting

**Windows SmartScreen: "Windows protected your PC"**
The app is new and not yet digitally signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Antivirus blocks the app (Avast, AVG, etc.)**
Some antivirus programs flag new unsigned apps by mistake. To fix it:
1. Uninstall StudyTracker.
2. Add this folder to your antivirus exceptions:
   `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\StudyTracker`
3. Install StudyTracker again.

**The app shows an error window**
Please send the file `%APPDATA%\StudyTracker\error.log`
to [@studytracker_app](https://www.instagram.com/studytracker_app/) on Instagram, or open an issue here on GitHub.
